🏠 Properties
Flats, plots, land — rental income, tax & Section 24 summary
🏢 Flat / Apartment — Due Diligence
  • Verify RERA registration number on state RERA portal
  • Check builder track record & delivery history
  • Confirm Completion Certificate (CC) / OC issued
  • Verify title deed — free of encumbrance (EC from sub-registrar)
  • Check approved building plan matches actual construction
  • Confirm khata / property tax registration in builder/seller name
  • Check home loan eligibility — bank technical valuation
  • Stamp duty + registration: ~5–7% of agreement value (state-specific)
  • Society maintenance + sinking fund — get last 3 years accounts
  • Confirm GST rate: 5% (under-construction), exempt (OC obtained)
🌱 Plot / Land — Due Diligence
  • Obtain and verify patta / record of rights (RoR) / khata
  • Check conversion: agricultural land needs NA (non-agricultural) order
  • Confirm survey number on government land records portal
  • Get Encumbrance Certificate (EC) for 30 years minimum
  • Verify no revenue dues / Betterment Charges pending
  • Check layout approval from HMDA / BDA / local authority
  • Confirm UDS (Undivided Share) matches sale deed if gated layout
  • Enquire about CLU (Change of Land Use) if planning construction
  • Check proximity to water bodies / low-lying areas (flood risk)
  • Stamp duty + registration for plots: 5–7% (state-specific)
📊 Key Valuation Metrics
Gross YieldAnnual Rent ÷ Property Value × 100
Healthy: ≥3% in metros, ≥4% in Tier-2
Price-to-RentPrice ÷ Annual Rent
Under 20× = buy; 20–30× = neutral; 30×+ = overpriced
EMI / IncomeKeep EMI ≤ 40% of net monthly income
Total CostBase price + stamp duty + registration + GST + society charges + interior
💸 Tax Implications
  • Home Loan Interest (Sec 24b): Up to ₹2L deduction (self-occupied); unlimited for let-out
  • Principal repayment (Sec 80C): Up to ₹1.5L within the 80C limit
  • LTCG on sale: >2 years held → 12.5% (no indexation post Jul 2024 budget)
  • STCG on sale: ≤2 years → taxed at slab rate
  • Reinvestment exemption (Sec 54): Buy new residential property within 2 years to defer LTCG
  • Annual Value (let-out): 30% standard deduction on net rent (Sec 24a)

Section 24 — Income from House Property

GAV = Annual rent received  |  NAV = GAV − Municipal taxes paid
Sec 24(a) standard deduction = 30% of NAV (for maintenance, covers repairs etc.)
Sec 24(b) = Home loan interest (up to ₹2L for self-occupied; unlimited for let-out)
Net income from house property = NAV − 30% − loan interest

📌 Section 80C — ₹1.5L limit
  • ELSS Mutual Funds (best for equity exposure + lock-in 3y)
  • PPF — 15-year tenure, EEE tax status, 7.1% interest
  • NPS Tier-I (own contribution) — up to ₹1.5L within 80C
  • Life Insurance Premium (traditional/term)
  • Home Loan Principal repayment
  • Sukanya Samriddhi / NSC / SCSS / Tax-saver FD (5yr)
💊 Section 80D — Health Insurance
  • Self + family (below 60): up to ₹25,000
  • Parents (below 60): additional ₹25,000 = total ₹50,000
  • Parents (senior citizen): additional ₹50,000 = total ₹75,000
  • Preventive health check-up: ₹5,000 within limits
  • Note: 80D benefit available in both Old & New regimes (partially)
🏛️ Section 80CCD(1B) — NPS Bonus
  • Additional NPS Tier-I investment: up to ₹50,000 over & above 80C limit
  • Effective saving: ₹15,600 at 30% slab + cess
  • Available under Old Tax Regime only
  • Employer NPS contribution (80CCD(2)): available in New Regime too
🏠 Home Loan Deductions
  • Sec 24(b): Interest up to ₹2L (self-occupied property)
  • Sec 80C: Principal up to ₹1.5L (within 80C limit)
  • First home buyers — additional ₹50,000 u/s 80EEA (if stamp duty ≤45L)
  • For let-out property: full interest deductible under Sec 24
  • Available in Old Regime; not in New Regime (except 80CCD2)
